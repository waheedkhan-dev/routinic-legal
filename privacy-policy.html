Routinic is built on one principle: your personal data belongs to you. This policy explains clearly what information is involved when you use the app, how it is handled, and what choices you have.

Data We Collect
Section 01

Routinic collects the minimum data necessary to run its features. Here is exactly what is stored and where:

  • Habit data: Habits you create, schedules, completion records, streaks, and Awesome Day history — stored locally on your device only.
  • Timer session data: Duration and completion state of timed habits (meditation, focus, workouts) — stored locally on your device only.
  • App preferences: Your chosen theme (Light, Dark, Dark Blue), reminder times, and settings — stored locally on your device only.
  • Ad consent choice: Whether you accept or decline personalised advertising via Google's UMP at first launch — stored on your device and shared with AdMob as required.

We do not collect your name, email address, location, or any identifying information. No account is required to use Routinic.

Local Storage — Your Data Stays on Your Device
Section 02

100% Offline. All habit data is stored locally on your Android device using an offline database. It is never sent to our servers — we do not operate servers for user data. We cannot access, view, or recover your data.

  • Your habit history is private by default — no one else can see it.
  • The app works fully without an internet connection.
  • If you uninstall Routinic or clear app data, your information is permanently deleted. We cannot recover it.
  • We recommend enabling Android's automatic device backup to protect against accidental loss.
How We Use Your Information
Section 03

Since all personal data stays on your device, it is used solely to power app features:

  • Displaying your habits, completion history, and streaks on screen
  • Calculating statistics, bar-chart analytics, calendar heatmap, and Awesome Days
  • Firing local reminders and alarms at the times you configure
  • Running the built-in countdown timer for timed habits
  • Applying your selected theme and display preferences

We do not use your data for profiling, marketing, or any purpose beyond running the app on your device.

Advertising & Ad Consent (Google AdMob)
Section 04

Routinic is free to download and use. To support ongoing development, the app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Consent is requested at first launch. When you first open Routinic, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) will ask whether you consent to personalised advertising. You may accept or decline. To change your choice later, go to your Android Google account settings under Ads & data, or reinstall the app to see the prompt again.

  • If you accept personalised ads: Google AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (GAID) to show relevant ads, governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • If you decline personalised ads: You will still see ads, but not targeted ones. No advertising identifier is used.
  • Routinic does not receive or store any data from the ad network.
  • All ad-related data handling is performed by Google AdMob under their own privacy terms.

Review Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Third-Party Services
Section 05
  • Google AdMob: Displays in-app advertisements. May collect device identifiers for ad delivery. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services: Required for standard Android functionality. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): Manages ad consent in compliance with GDPR and CCPA. No habit data is shared with UMP.

No other analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs are used in Routinic.

App Permissions Explained
Section 06

Routinic requests only the permissions it genuinely needs:

PermissionNameWhy it is needed
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Notifications To send habit reminders at your chosen times. Disable anytime in Android Settings → Apps → Routinic → Notifications.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Exact Alarms To fire reminders at the precise time you set, including across Daylight Saving Time changes. Required on Android 12+.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Boot Receiver To reschedule your reminders automatically after your device restarts so alarms are not lost.
INTERNET Internet Required by Google AdMob to load advertisements. Not used to transmit your habit data.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Network State Used by AdMob to check connectivity before loading ads. Not used to access habit data.
Data Security
Section 07

Because your data never leaves your device, its security depends on your device's own protections. We recommend:

  • Keeping your Android OS and security patches up to date
  • Using a secure lock screen — PIN, fingerprint, or face unlock
  • Enabling Android's automatic backup to protect against accidental data loss
Children's Privacy
Section 08

Routinic is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because no account is required and all data is stored locally, no personal information is collected from any user regardless of age. If you have concerns about a child using the app, please reach out using the details in Section 11.

Your Rights and Choices
Section 09

Because all data is stored locally, you have full control at all times:

  • Access your data: All data is visible directly within the app.
  • Delete your data: Delete individual habits in the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Routinic → Storage → Clear data to remove everything.
  • Change your ad consent: Go to your Android Google account settings under Ads & data, or reinstall the app to see the UMP consent prompt again.
  • Disable notifications: Android Settings → Apps → Routinic → Notifications at any time.
  • Opt out of personalised ads globally: Android Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID.
Changes to This Policy
Section 10

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. When we make significant changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and note changes in the Play Store update description. Continued use of Routinic after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
